How I work

From problem to product, in six steps

The same path on every project, so nothing gets lost between the business and the build.

  1. 01

    Problem

    Start from the business reality (the friction, the cost, the thing that isn't working) before touching a solution.

  2. 02

    Discovery

    Interviews, workshops and stakeholder conversations to surface what people actually need, not just what they ask for.

  3. 03

    Analysis

    Structure the ambiguity (process maps, data, edge cases) until the shape of the problem is unmistakable.

  4. 04

    Requirements

    BRD / FSD / SRS, UML and user stories with acceptance criteria tight enough to leave no room for interpretation.

  5. 05

    System

    Align engineering, QA and stakeholders around one definition of done, and steward every sprint toward it.

  6. 06

    Product

    Through UAT to a clean release, then validate the outcome with data and prove the impact.
